SAMPLE SALES DOCUMENT - Objection Handling Techniques

This is a sample sales document for testing the VoiceCoach V2 application.

KEY TECHNIQUES:

1. The Feel, Felt, Found Method
When a prospect expresses concern, respond with:
"I understand how you feel. Many of our clients felt the same way initially. However, what they found was that this solution actually saved them money in the long run."

2. Price Anchoring
Instead of dropping price immediately, redirect to value:
"I understand budget is important. Let me ask - if this solution could increase your revenue by 25%, what would that be worth to your business?"

3. The Assumptive Close
"Based on everything we've discussed, it sounds like this addresses your main challenges. When would you like to get started?"

4. Building Urgency
"This pricing is only available through the end of the month. After that, we'll need to move to our standard pricing structure."

5. Social Proof
"Companies similar to yours, like [specific example], saw a 40% improvement in their metrics within the first quarter."

OBJECTION RESPONSES:

"It's too expensive"
→ "I understand cost is a concern. Can you help me understand what specifically feels expensive about it? Is it the total investment or the monthly payment?"

"We need to think about it"
→ "I completely understand. Thinking it through is important. What specific aspects would you like to discuss further?"

"We're not ready right now"
→ "That makes sense. When you say 'not ready,' what would need to change for this to be a priority?"

Remember: Always listen first, acknowledge their concern, then provide a thoughtful response that moves the conversation forward.
