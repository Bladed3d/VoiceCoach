Sales Outline for Golf Coaching Package
Below is a brief sales script outline for selling the $6,000/year professional golf coaching package. The script is structured around the provided sales stages, focusing on aligning the prospect with the salesperson in MEFS (Mentally, Emotionally, Financially, Schedule) areas. It assumes a phone or in-person conversation. Each stage includes key talking points and sample dialogue to guide the flow toward a natural sale.

Stage 1: Rapport - Lay a foundation for roles and who I am
Build trust and connection by introducing yourself, your expertise, and setting the tone as a helpful guide rather than a pushy seller.

"Hi [Prospect's Name], thanks for taking the time to chat today. I'm [Your Name], a certified golf coaching specialist with over 10 years helping players from beginners to pros improve their game. I've worked with coaches who've trained legends like Tiger Woods, and my role here is to listen to your goals and see if our program aligns with what you're looking for. How's your day going so far?"

Stage 2: Problem Intro - Why are we talking?
Introduce the common pain points in golf coaching to establish relevance and get the prospect nodding in agreement.

"The reason we're chatting is that many golfers, like you, are frustrated with inconsistent swings or putting that hold them back from enjoying the game or lowering their scores. Whether it's mental blocks during rounds, emotional frustration from poor performance, financial waste on ineffective lessons, or scheduling conflicts with local coaches, these issues can make golf feel more like a chore than a passion. What specifically brought you to explore better coaching options?"

Stage 3: Solution Intro - How can we make this better?
High-level overview of the package as a superior alternative, teasing benefits without diving deep.

"Our professional golf coaching package is designed to transform that. For $6,000 a year, you get unlimited video analysis of your swing or putting from expert coaches, plus a 4-day immersive live session with a world-renowned pro—the same level that helped Tiger Woods. This isn't generic advice; it's personalized to fix your unique issues and help you play better, faster."

Stage 4: Problem Application - How does this problem apply to prospect (MEFS)?
Probe how the general problem impacts them personally across MEFS to build emotional connection.

"Let's talk about how this plays out for you. Mentally, does inconsistent coaching leave you second-guessing your shots on the course? Emotionally, how frustrating is it when you don't see progress after lessons? Financially, have you spent money on local coaches without real results? And on your schedule, does traveling to sessions disrupt your routine? I'd love to hear more about that."

Stage 5: What is their solution now? MEFS
Explore their current approach to highlight gaps, using MEFS to quantify dissatisfaction.

"What are you doing now for coaching? Mentally, does it build your confidence, or leave you doubting? Emotionally, does it make golf more enjoyable, or add stress? Financially, is it cost-effective, or are you overpaying for minimal gains? Schedule-wise, how does it fit into your life—easy or a hassle?"

Stage 6: How can we make a difference? MEFS (easier, faster, better, happier, $$)
Contrast the package's benefits against their current solution, emphasizing improvements in MEFS.

"Here's how we can make a real difference: Mentally, our pros provide clear, Tiger Woods-level strategies to build unshakable focus—faster than trial-and-error. Emotionally, you'll feel the joy of consistent improvement, turning frustration into excitement. Financially, at $6,000 for a full year (just $500/month), it's better value than ongoing local fees without results—saving you money long-term. Schedule-wise, video submissions fit your time, and the 4-day live session is planned around you, making it easier and happier overall."

Stage 7: What would their ideal solution look like? (MEFS)
Let them describe their dream outcome to align visions and reveal desires.

"If you could design the perfect coaching solution, what would it include? Mentally, how would it sharpen your game mindset? Emotionally, what would make you happiest on the course? Financially, what value would justify the investment? Schedule-wise, how flexible would it need to be?"

Stage 8: Where are we NOT aligned?
Address any misalignments head-on to resolve objections and confirm fit.

"Based on what you've shared, it sounds like we're aligned on mental clarity, emotional satisfaction, and scheduling ease—but maybe not fully on the financial side yet? What concerns do you have there, or anywhere else, so we can address them?"

Stage 9: Sale
Close naturally once alignment is achieved, confirming commitment.

"Great, with us aligned mentally on building your confidence, emotionally on reigniting your passion, financially on the $6,000 delivering superior value, and schedule-wise with flexible access—it seems like a natural fit. Shall we get you started with the package today? I can walk you through enrollment right now."
