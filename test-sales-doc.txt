Sales Script for Enterprise Software Solutions

PROSPECT QUALIFICATION:
- Ask about current software challenges and pain points
- Identify decision makers and budget authority 
- Understand timeline for implementation
- Discover compliance or security requirements

KEY VALUE PROPOSITIONS:
- 50% reduction in manual processes
- ROI typically achieved within 6 months
- Enterprise-grade security and compliance
- 24/7 customer support included

OBJECTION HANDLING:
Budget Concerns: "Let's discuss the cost of NOT solving this problem"
Implementation Time: "Our proven onboarding process gets you live in 30 days"
Security Concerns: "We're SOC 2 certified with bank-level encryption"

CLOSING TECHNIQUES:
- Trial close: "How does this solution address your main concerns?"
- Assumptive close: "When would be ideal for implementation?"
- Urgency close: "We have a special promotion ending this quarter"

COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGES:
- Only solution with built-in analytics dashboard
- Fastest implementation time in the market
- Highest customer satisfaction ratings
