# Advanced Sales Strategy Document

## Executive Summary
This document outlines proven consultative selling techniques for enterprise accounts over $50K.

## Key Methodologies

### SPIN Selling Framework
- **Situation Questions**: Understand current state
- **Problem Questions**: Identify pain points  
- **Implication Questions**: Explore consequences
- **Need-Payoff Questions**: Build value proposition

### Objection Handling Matrix

#### Price Objections
1. Acknowledge the concern
2. Redirect to value conversation
3. Use cost-of-inaction analysis
4. Present ROI calculations

#### Authority Objections
1. Identify true decision makers
2. Multi-threaded selling approach
3. Champion development strategies

### Closing Techniques
- Assumptive close for qualified prospects
- Alternative choice close
- Trial close throughout discovery
- Value confirmation before final ask

## Implementation Guidelines
- Focus on business outcomes, not features
- Quantify impact with specific metrics
- Use customer success stories as proof points
- Maintain consultative approach throughout

## Success Metrics
- Increase close rates by 20%
- Reduce sales cycle by 25% 
- Maintain premium pricing on 90% of deals
