
 Sales Outline for Golf Coaching Package

Below is a brief sales script outline for selling the $6,000/year professional golf coaching package. The script is structured around the provided sales stages, focusing on aligning the prospect with the salesperson in MEFS (Mentally, Emotionally, Financially, Schedule) areas. It assumes a phone or in-person conversation. Each stage includes key talking points and sample dialogue to guide the flow toward a natural sale.

This sales script includes examples of how tracking for MEFS Alignment (Mental, Emotional, Financial, Schedule) can work where the user can be prompted to ask Calibrated questions, use mirroring to get more insights, and build rapport with proactive validation and other tools. Each element that pertains to a MEFS component is designated with (M) for mental, (E) for emotional, etc.

Stage 1: Rapport - Lay a foundation for roles and who I am
Build trust and connection by introducing yourself, your expertise, and setting the tone as a helpful guide rather than a pushy seller.

"Hi [Prospect's Name], thanks for taking the time to chat today. I'm [Your Name], a certified golf coaching specialist with over 10 years helping players from beginners to pros improve their game. I've worked with coaches who've trained legends like Tiger Woods, and my role here is to listen to your goals and see if our program aligns with what you're looking for. So tell me about your golf game."

Stage 1 objective: Learn about them, their wants, their fears, invite them to brag about successes and disclose fears (in a playful way).

Tools: Mirroring: 50%, Proactive Validation: 15%, Calibrated Questions: 25%, We not I: 10%
MEFS examples: 
Mirroring (M) They say, "I am unhappy with my short game. I got a new putter but it didn't help." You respond, "got a new putter?"

Proactive Validation (E) They say, "I am tired of having the worst score with my friends." You respond, "I agree, and they won't shut up with the jokes at your expense, right?"

Calibrated Question: (F) They say, "I tried a local coach but it didn't work out well." You respond, "What was your budget for your coach?"

Note: ideal transition to next stage is using tool: Buy-In/We. "Is it OK if WE discuss 3 of the most common problems we help golfers overcome?"

Stage 2: Problem Intro - Why are we talking?
Introduce the common pain points in golf coaching, then focus on the prospect's specific pain and problems.

"The reason we're chatting is that many golfers, like you, are frustrated (E) with inconsistent swings or putting that hold them back from enjoying the game or lowering their scores. Whether it's mental blocks (M) during rounds, emotional frustration from poor performance, financial waste (F) on ineffective lessons, or scheduling conflicts with local coaches (S), these issues can make golf feel more like a chore than a passion. What specifically brought you to explore better coaching options?"

Emotional Response Validation: (E) They say, "I am tired of paying for no results. My coach is an idiot." You respond, "Certainly isn't fair to pay for something you aren't getting."

Stage 3: Solution Intro - (Buy-In) Is it OK if we discuss the 5 benefits *WE* give our members?

"Our professional golf coaching package is designed to transform your game. For $6,124, you get unlimited video analysis of your swing and putting form from your expert coach, plus a 4-day immersive live session with a world-renowned pro—the same level that helped Tiger Woods. This isn't generic advice; it's personalized to fix your unique issues and help you play better, faster."

Accusation Audit (F) Say, "At first you might think that 6 grand is a lot of money, but 68% of our customers feel it saves them time and money, and lots of frustration, over amateur coaches that take your money for no results.

Calibrated Question (EF) Say, "Last month a client of ours won $9k at $1,000 per hole bets against his buddies. Smiling ear to ear he said, 'best investment in myself I have ever made.' How difficult would it be fore you get get away for 4 days next month to play and learn from a pro golf coach?

Stage 4: Problem Application - How does this problem apply to prospect (MEFS)?
Calibrated Question: (E) Say, "5 weeks ago a client said, 'If I knew how good it would feel to finally play this game I love at a higher level, I would have signed up years ago!' How would you feel to finally get the improvement to your short game that you deserve?

"Let's talk about how this plays out for you. (M) Does inconsistent coaching leave you second-guessing your shots on the course? (E) How frustrating is it when you don't see progress after lessons? (F) *WE* want you to be done spending money on local coaches without real results! I have an opening with the world famous Mike Zork in Tampa Florida next month or a fun weekend with him in the Bahamas in November. Which works best for your schedule?" (Being setting the stage for the close)

Stage 6: How can we make a difference? MEFS (easier, faster, better, happier, $$)
Contrast the package's benefits against their current solution, emphasizing improvements in MEFS.

"Here's how we can make a real difference: (M) Mentally, our pros provide clear, Tiger Woods-level strategies to build unshakable focus—faster than trial-and-error. (E) Emotionally, you'll feel the joy of consistent improvement, turning frustration into excitement. (F) Financially, at $6,000 for a full year (just $500/month), it's better value than ongoing local fees without results—saving you money long-term. (S) Schedule-wise, video submissions fit your time, and the 4-day live session is planned around you, making it easier and happier overall."

Stage 7: What would their ideal solution look like? (MEFS)
Let them describe their dream outcome to align visions and reveal desires.

"If you could design the perfect coaching solution, what would it include? Mentally, how would it sharpen your game mindset? Emotionally, what would make you happiest on the course? Financially, what value would justify the investment? Schedule-wise, how flexible would it need to be?"

Stage 8: Where are we NOT aligned?
Prospect says, "I am just not sure."

Take Away/DJ Voice: Say, "John (name) if it is not completely obvious that this is exactly what you have been looking for to finally improve your golf game and feel great every time you play, then don't do it!

Extended Silence. Let them talk. (Note: you had your chance to sell them. Give them a chance to sell themselves. You talking more is not going to save the deal unless they tell you what is the real problem.)

Eventually say, "John (name) I am not going to let you do this unless you can convince me that you are excited about it, can afford it, and will enjoy golf more than ever have a year long pro instructor.

Stage 9: Sale
Close naturally once alignment is achieved, confirming commitment.

"Great, with us aligned mentally on building your confidence, emotionally on reigniting your passion, financially on the $6,000 delivering superior value, and schedule-wise with flexible access—it seems like a natural fit. Shall we get you started with the package today? I can walk you through enrollment right now."
