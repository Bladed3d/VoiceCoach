Generic Sales Outline Template
This is a customizable template based on the Alignment Sales Process. It uses the provided sales stages to guide prospects toward alignment in MEFS (Mentally, Emotionally, Financially, Schedule) areas, leading to a natural sale. Replace placeholders (e.g., [Your Name], [Product/Service], [Price]) with your specific details. The script assumes a phone or in-person conversation but can be adapted.
Stage 1: Rapport - Lay a foundation for roles and who I am
Build trust by introducing yourself, your expertise, and positioning as a helpful guide.

"Hi [Prospect's Name], thanks for taking the time to chat today. I'm [Your Name], a [Your Role/Expertise] with [Your Experience/Company Background]. My role here is to listen to your goals and see if our [Product/Service] aligns with what you're looking for. How's your day going so far?"

Stage 2: Problem Intro - Why are we talking?
Introduce common pain points related to your industry to establish relevance.

"The reason we're chatting is that many [Target Audience, e.g., businesses/individuals] like you face [Common Problems, e.g., inefficiencies, frustrations]. Whether it's mental [e.g., overwhelm], emotional [e.g., stress], financial [e.g., wasted costs], or scheduling [e.g., time conflicts], these issues can hold you back from [Desired Outcome, e.g., growth/enjoyment]. What specifically brought you to explore solutions like this?"

Stage 3: Solution Intro - How can we make this better?
Provide a high-level overview of your offering, teasing benefits.

"Our [Product/Service] is designed to address that. For [Price/Structure, e.g., $X per year/month], you get [Key Features/Benefits, e.g., personalized support, tools, sessions]. This isn't generic—it's tailored to [Unique Value, e.g., deliver faster results]."

Stage 4: Problem Application - How does this problem apply to prospect (MEFS)?
Probe how the general problem impacts them personally across MEFS.

"Let's talk about how this plays out for you. Mentally, does [General Problem] leave you [e.g., second-guessing decisions]? Emotionally, how [e.g., frustrating] is it? Financially, have you [e.g., spent money without results]? And on your schedule, does it [e.g., disrupt your routine]? I'd love to hear more about that."

Stage 5: What is their solution now? MEFS
Explore their current approach to highlight gaps.

"What are you using/doing now for [Related Need]? Mentally, does it [e.g., build confidence]? Emotionally, does it [e.g., reduce stress]? Financially, is it [e.g., cost-effective]? Schedule-wise, how does it fit—[e.g., easy or a hassle]?"

Stage 6: How can we make a difference? MEFS (easier, faster, better, happier, $$)
Contrast your offering's benefits against their current solution.

"Here's how we can make a real difference: Mentally, our [Product/Service] provides [e.g., clear strategies]—faster than [Current Drawback]. Emotionally, you'll [e.g., feel more satisfied]. Financially, at [Price], it's better value than [Current Issue]—saving you [e.g., money long-term]. Schedule-wise, [e.g., flexible access] makes it easier and happier overall."

Stage 7: What would their ideal solution look like? (MEFS)
Let them describe their dream outcome to align visions.

"If you could design the perfect [Product/Service], what would it include? Mentally, how would it [e.g., sharpen your focus]? Emotionally, what would make you happiest? Financially, what value would justify the investment? Schedule-wise, how flexible would it need to be?"

Stage 8: Where are we NOT aligned?
Address misalignments to resolve objections.

"Based on what you've shared, it sounds like we're aligned on [e.g., mental clarity, emotional satisfaction]—but maybe not fully on [e.g., the financial side]? What concerns do you have there, or anywhere else, so we can address them?"

Stage 9: Sale
Close once alignment is achieved.

"Great, with us aligned mentally on [e.g., building confidence], emotionally on [e.g., reducing stress], financially on [e.g., the value], and schedule-wise with [e.g., flexibility]—it seems like a natural fit. Shall we get you started with [Product/Service] today? I can walk you through enrollment right now."
